How to get help

If you have questions about app behavior, ads, privacy prompts, content updates, questions and explanations, mock exams, mistake review, saved questions, progress records, the eBook, glossary, or licensing guide content, please contact support by email.

  • A screenshot or screen recording, if available

About the app

Real Estate Exam Pass is a bilingual English-Chinese study app for California and New York Salesperson License Exam preparation. It includes topic practice, mock exams, review tools, progress records, an eBook, glossary, and licensing guide content. It is a study aid and is not an official government, licensing board, or exam vendor app.
