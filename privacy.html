Real Estate Pass

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Support

Real Estate Pass is a bilingual study app for real estate salesperson exam preparation. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may use, how it may be handled, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

What this app does

Real Estate Pass helps users prepare for the California and New York real estate salesperson exams with bilingual English-Chinese practice questions, explanations, mock exams, mistake review, saved questions, progress records, an exam eBook, glossary, and licensing guide content.

Information that may be used

  • Learning activity such as answer history, mistakes, saved questions, mock exam results, topic progress, selected state track, language preference, and study display preference
  • Device or app usage information needed for app stability, diagnostics, troubleshooting, advertising consent, or content delivery
  • Advertising-related information when ads are shown in the app, including identifiers that may be available under your platform settings and consent choices
  • Network request information handled by hosting providers when the app checks for supported remote study content updates
  • Email address and details you choose to send if you contact support

Information we do not ask for

The app does not require an account. It does not ask you to enter your legal name, home address, Social Security number, real estate license application number, payment card information, or property transaction details.

How information is used

  • To save study progress and support review features
  • To show mistakes, saved questions, mock exam history, and topic progress
  • To deliver built-in or remote study content and keep supported question banks current
  • To understand app performance, diagnose issues, and fix bugs
  • To show ads and support a free version of the app
  • To respond when you contact support

Local storage

Study data such as attempts, mistakes, saved questions, mock exam results, progress, state selection, language selection, and preferences are primarily stored on your device. You can remove locally stored study data by deleting the app or clearing app data through your device settings where available.

Third-party services

The app may use third-party services for advertising, consent collection, diagnostics, analytics, platform services, or content delivery. These services may process device identifiers, advertising consent signals, IP address, or technical usage data under their own policies.

Advertising and tracking

The app may display ads. On iOS, the app may request App Tracking Transparency permission before using data for tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. On Android, the app may use the advertising ID when allowed by your device settings and applicable consent choices. You can manage these choices in your device privacy or advertising settings.

Remote content updates

When remote content updates are enabled, the app may check a remote manifest and download supported encrypted study bundles. These requests are used to provide updated study material and may be logged by hosting infrastructure in the ordinary course of providing the service.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for real estate exam study and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support contact, please email us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be identified by the date shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: app.support.us@gmail.com
